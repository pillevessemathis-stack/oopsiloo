Privacy policy

Short Generator, by the Oopsiloo Cartoons team. Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Who uses the app

Short Generator is a private tool used only by the owner of the Oopsiloo Cartoons YouTube channel. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not collect data from viewers of our videos.

Google user data we access

When the channel owner connects their Google account, Short Generator requests:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish the videos the owner has approved on the Oopsiloo Cartoons channel.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to check the channel name and confirm each video's settings after upload.
  • Manage the YouTube account (youtube): only to create the channel's playlists and add our own videos to them.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read our channel's views, watch time and subscribers, and decide which stories to make next.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to run the Oopsiloo Cartoons channel, for the channel owner.
  • The Google access token and the channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server of ours.
  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Short summaries of our own channel's statistics may be sent to an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude API) to get planning advice. They never include any viewer's personal data.

Short Generator's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Our videos are made for children, but Short Generator never collects any information from children or from any viewer. Our videos are published as "made for kids" on YouTube, which turns off comments and personalized ads.

Removing access

The channel owner can disconnect the channel at any time in Short Generator, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The local copy of the data can be deleted at any time.
